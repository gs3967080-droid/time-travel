# Third-Party Historical Map Data

The historical boundary files under `src/data/historicalMaps/` are extracted from:

- **江山时序 Jiangshan Atlas** — https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map
- Copyright © 2026 DeanChensj
- License: GNU General Public License v3.0, for the selected polity and
  first-level administrative-region layers.

Only the `polity` and `region` layers are included. The restricted
prefecture-level CHGIS/Hartwell layers are not included or redistributed.

The source project describes these polygons as schematic historical
reconstructions checked against historical texts, not surveyed borders.
They should not be cited as precise or authoritative geographic data.

Selected snapshots:

- 秦: `s_qin_a`, 前 221—前 215
- 唐: `s_tang_b0a`, 755—757
- 清: `s_qing_e`, 1796—1851
